E-Series Round Steel Picnic Table

Entirely Affordable

Durable and economical, these E-Series round picnic tables provide stand out service for both indoor and outdoor environments at a sound price.

Dimensions
Model 358-RDEV - 83"Dia. x 30"H
Model 358H-RDEV - 83"L x 62-1/2"W x 30"H

The sturdy 46" round table top and 10" wide bench seats feature a low maintenance diamond pattern which is supported by an MIG welded, 1-5/8"O.D. steel tube frame. Entirely constructed utilizing commercial grade steel, finished in a trusted polyethylene coating, these cost-effective tables are built ready for high-volume use.
