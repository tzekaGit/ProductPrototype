Rectangular Wood Shelter Tables


Model 238H-4-PT10

Popular Walk Through Design

Wooden shelter picnic tables create seating for everyone while maximizing facility space. Classically styled, the 10' and 12' table features wood planks constructed with No. 1 Southern Yellow Pine, which ensures a smooth, natural sturdy surface. This shelter table is ideal for indoor or outdoor for large group gatherings location—popular for sun shelters, gazebos and festivals.

Model 238-3-T10

ADA compliant shelter tables create independence, comfort, and inclusion for all abilities while maximizing outdoor space. Classically styled, the wood shelter table is available in a 10' length with a three frame and a 12' with four frame design

The highly popular walk through design, tailors to all individuals by allowing easy and convenient maneuvering in, out and around the table. MIG welded for superior stability the unique frame is constructed using 2-3/8"O.D. steel tubing and 1-5/8"O.D. support braces and finished in a weather resistant, UV stabilized powder-coating.

The three frame design of the 10' model has a 20-3/4" dual side table overhang for simple and accessible seating for two wheelchairs. The innovative four frame is designed with separate bench seats creating two accessible center seating options along with the two accessible table ends.

Universal Access
Wheelchair and Mobility Device access rectangular outdoor tables conform to current ADA specifications/requirements.
