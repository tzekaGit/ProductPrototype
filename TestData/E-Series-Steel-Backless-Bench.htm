E-Series Flat Bench

Durability, Made Even More Affordable

Built for resiliency and made for enjoyment, the E-Series flat park bench features a 10" diamond pattern seat plank, reinforced with a flat steel brace and supported by a heavy-duty 2-3/8"O.D. round steel tube frame. Combining all-steel construction, open diamond seat pattern and durable, non-corrosive thermoplastic, powder-coated finish provides lasting protection against the elements.

Dimensions
6' Bench - 72"L x 10"W* x 18-1/4"H♦
8' Bench - 96"L x 10"W* x 18-1/4"H♦
*Portable Frame Base is 22-1/4"W
♦In-ground Mount is From Grade when Installed

Designed to fit a variety of applications, the E-Series 6' and 8' flat bench is available in portable, surface or in-ground mounting options.
